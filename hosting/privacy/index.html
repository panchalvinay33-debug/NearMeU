Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 July 2026

1. About NearMeU

NearMeU is an Android application for adult users to discover people nearby and communicate through private one-to-one messaging. This policy explains how information is collected, used, protected and deleted.

2. Information we process

  • Account information such as sign-in identity and authentication records.
  • Profile information chosen by the user, including nickname, age, gender, preferences and profile photo.
  • Approximate location information used to provide nearby discovery. NearMeU is designed not to publicly display a user's exact location.
  • Private chat content including text, replies, emoji, photos, compressed videos and voice notes.
  • Safety and moderation information such as blocks, reports, suspension state and related records.
  • Device and technical information required for notifications, App Check, security, crash diagnostics, analytics and performance monitoring.

3. How information is used

  • To create and manage accounts and profiles.
  • To provide nearby discovery, private messaging and notifications.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate reports and enforce safety rules.
  • To secure the service, diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect users and the service.

4. Storage and retention

NearMeU uses Firebase services for authentication, cloud data, temporary private media, notifications and backend operations. Private chat history is designed to be local-first and stored in the app's private encrypted storage after successful delivery. Cloud chat messages and private media are subject to limited retention and cleanup processes, currently designed around a seven-day cloud retention window where applicable. Some records may be retained longer where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups or unresolved moderation matters.

5. Sharing

NearMeU does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app, including Google Firebase services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to investigate abuse or to protect users and the service.

6. User controls

Users can edit profile information, block or report other users, manage notification preferences and request account deletion. Deletion instructions are available on the Account Deletion page.

7. Security

NearMeU uses measures including authenticated access, Firebase security rules, trusted backend operations, App Check, screenshot protection on protected screens and encrypted app-private local storage. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their account and device.

8. Age requirement

NearMeU is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. The service is not directed to children.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to supportnearmeu@gmail.com.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
