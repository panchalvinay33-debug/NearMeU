Delete your NearMeU account

Use either method below. NearMeU may ask you to verify ownership before completing a request.

Method 1 — Delete inside the app

  1. Open NearMeU and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Complete any requested reauthentication and confirm deletion.

This is the fastest and most secure method because the request is made from the signed-in account.

Method 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support from the address linked to your NearMeU account. Include your registered email address and state that you want the account deleted.

Email account deletion request

supportnearmeu@gmail.com

What is deleted

After verification and completion, NearMeU removes or schedules removal of the account, profile, authentication-linked application data and associated service data in accordance with the app's deletion process. Temporary cloud chat media and messages are also subject to their normal retention and cleanup rules.

Some limited information may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, safety investigations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security logs or backup integrity. Data stored only on another user's device, such as a message already downloaded to that user's encrypted local history, may not be remotely removable from that device.

Processing time

Email requests are normally acknowledged within 24–48 hours. Verification or legally required retention may affect the final completion time.
